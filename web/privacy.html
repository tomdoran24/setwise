Privacy Policy

Overview

What this policy covers

Setwise is a concert setlist prediction app that helps you prepare for live shows. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

We collect only what we need to provide the service. We do not sell your data.

Data collection

Information we collect

When you use Setwise, we may collect the following:

Account information

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. This is used to identify your account and is stored securely in our database.

Spotify connection

If you connect your Spotify account, we store an access token and refresh token that allow us to create playlists on your behalf. We do not store your Spotify password. We do not access your listening history or personal playlists.

YouTube connection

If you connect your YouTube account, we store a token that allows us to create playlists on your behalf. We do not access your viewing history or existing playlists.

Usage data

We may log basic request data (artist searches, setlist predictions) to improve prediction accuracy and diagnose errors. This is not linked to identifiable personal information.

How we use it

Purpose of data collection

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the Setwise service
  • Create Spotify and YouTube playlists on your behalf when requested
  • Maintain your account and preferences across sessions
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating Setwise.

Third parties

Services we work with

Setwise uses the following third-party services to function. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Spotify — playlist creation. Spotify Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube — sign-in and playlist creation. Google Privacy Policy
  • setlist.fm — historical setlist data
  • Ticketmaster — upcoming show data

Your data is not shared with these services beyond what is necessary to fulfil your requests (e.g. your Spotify token is sent to Spotify when creating a playlist).

Storage & security

How your data is stored

Your account data and OAuth tokens are stored in a secured PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway (EU/US infrastructure). Tokens are stored at rest and transmitted over HTTPS only.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to have your data deleted, contact us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.

Your rights

Control over your data

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Disconnect your Spotify or YouTube account at any time via your account settings in the app
  • Revoke Setwise's access to your Google account via Google Account Permissions
